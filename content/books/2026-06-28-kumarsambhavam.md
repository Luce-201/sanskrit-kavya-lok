description: "**Kumārasambhava** (The Birth of Kumāra) is a foundational epic
  poem (*Mahakavya*) in classical Sanskrit literature, composed by the legendary
  4th–5th century poet **Kalidasa**. The narrative centers on a cosmic crisis
  where the gods can only defeat the invincible demon Tarakasura through a child
  born to Lord Shiva and Parvati. The epic beautifully details Parvati's intense
  spiritual penance to win the heart of a detached, meditating Shiva—especially
  after the god of love, Kamadeva, is incinerated by Shiva's third eye for
  trying to interfere—culminating in their divine marriage and the birth of
  their warrior son, Kumāra (Kartikeya), who restores cosmic balance. Renowned
  for its lyrical beauty, vivid descriptions of the Himalayas, and deep
  exploration of love transitioning from physical attraction to spiritual
  devotion, it remains one of the finest masterpieces of Indian classical
  literature."
